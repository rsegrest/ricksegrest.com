Boids on the Beam

Flocking, drawn as beam geometry. Each boid is an arrowhead the beam traces in three strokes, pointed along its heading and brighter the faster it moves. Three simple rules — do not crowd, match your neighbours, drift toward the crowd — produce a flock nobody is steering. Watch the phosphor: with persistence turned up the trails merge and the flock reads as one flowing organism rather than a set of separate shapes.
